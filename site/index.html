Talk into any app on your PC. Your voice never leaves it.

How it works

Hold, speak, let go

If you can type there, you can talk there. The same three steps in mail, chats, documents and the browser.

  1. Right Ctrl
    1

    Hold your shortcut

    Click where the text should go and hold Right Ctrl. Any other key, key combination or extra mouse button works too.

  2. Listening Mail 0:02

    Thanks for the notes.

    2

    Speak

    Talk the way you normally would. Your words appear live in a small pill at the top of the screen, together with the name and icon of the app they go into.

  3. Inserted into Mail

    Hi Jonas,

    Thanks for the notes.

    3

    Let go

    The text is inserted where you were typing. Changed your mind? Press Esc instead and nothing is inserted.

Questions

Good to know

Each question has a short answer. Open it for the details.

Browse the code
Does Vorto work offline?Yes

Yes. Vorto only goes online for two things: to download a voice model once, from Hugging Face (a pinned version, checked with SHA-256), and to check GitHub for updates. Recognition runs entirely on your PC.

Does my voice leave my PC?No

No. Speech recognition runs 100% on your PC, and your audio and text never leave it. There's no account, nothing is uploaded and Vorto sends no telemetry.

Which apps does it work in?Any

Any Windows app where you can type: mail, chats, documents, the browser. Vorto pastes or types the text where you were typing. After pasting, your clipboard is restored. Apps running as administrator can block inserted text.

Can I use a different shortcut?Yes

Yes. Right Ctrl is the default. Any key, key combination or extra mouse button can be the shortcut, even a single key like Menu. Hold it while you talk, or use toggle: press once to start, again to finish.

Which languages does it understand?Up to 99

Parakeet v3 covers English and 24 more European languages. Whisper Turbo, Small and Base cover 99 languages.

Do I need a graphics card?No

No. Parakeet v3, the recommended model, runs on the processor: on a Ryzen 7 5700X3D it writes 12 seconds of speech in about 0.5 seconds. With a graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, the Whisper models run on it through Vulkan. Whisper Turbo needs one and takes about 0.7 seconds on an RTX 4060.

Does Vorto keep what I said?On your PC only

Vorto keeps your last 100 dictations on your PC so you can copy them again. Nothing is uploaded. Turn off Save history in Settings to keep nothing. Settings, history and voice models live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.vorto.desktop. If you uninstall with “Delete the application data”, they're removed too.

What does my PC need?Windows 10 or 11

Windows 11, or Windows 10 version 22H2, 64-bit. 8 GB of RAM is recommended, and about 1 GB of free storage for the app and the Parakeet model. You don't need a graphics card, administrator rights or an account. Vorto uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows 11 and current Windows 10. If it's missing, the installer adds it (that needs an internet connection).

Which processors work?Intel or AMD

A 64-bit Intel or AMD processor with AVX2: Intel Core 4th generation or newer, AMD Ryzen, or Intel N100. Older budget chips without AVX2, like the Celeron N4000, N4500 and N5100 or the Pentium Silver N5000 and N6000, aren't supported yet. Vorto shows a message there instead of crashing. On Windows 11 on Arm (Snapdragon), Vorto runs through the x64 emulation built into Windows, version 24H2 or later. That isn't tested yet and may be slower.

Why does Windows warn me?Not signed yet

Vorto isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC” when you run the installer. Select More info, then Run anyway. The source code and the build are public on GitHub.

How does it stay up to date?Automatically

Vorto checks GitHub for new versions, downloads them in the background and shows Restart to update. Updates are cryptographically signed. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings.

Is it open source?MIT

Yes, under the MIT license. Read the source code on GitHub.
