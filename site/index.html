Privacy

Your voice never
leaves your PC.

  • Speech recognition runs 100% on your PC. No account, no telemetry.
  • The microphone is off unless you're dictating.
  • Recordings are temporary files that Windows deletes as soon as they're closed.
  • AI editing is off by default. With Ollama or LM Studio nothing leaves your PC. Only if you allow an online provider does the text, never the voice, go there.
  • Otherwise online only for voice models (pinned, checked with SHA-256) and signed updates.
  • Open source under the MIT license. Every release is built on GitHub from its tagged source.

FAQ

Questions?

Everything else is on GitHub.

Does my voice leave my PC?

No. Speech recognition runs 100% on your PC. There's no account, nothing is uploaded and Vorto sends no telemetry. If you turn on AI editing with a cloud provider, only the recognized text goes to that provider. With Ollama or LM Studio, even that stays on your PC.

Does Vorto work offline?

Yes. Vorto only goes online to download a voice model once, from Hugging Face (a pinned version, checked with SHA-256), and to check GitHub for updates.

Which apps does it work in?

Any Windows app where you can type: mail, chats, documents, the browser. Vorto pastes or types the text where you were typing, and restores your clipboard afterwards. Apps running as administrator can block inserted text.

Is AI editing required?

No, it's off by default. Turn it on in the app, pick a provider and adjust the styles. Press Esc while the pill says Polishing to insert your words as spoken.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Parakeet v3 runs on the processor: on a Ryzen 7 5700X3D it writes 12 seconds of speech in about 0.5 seconds. With a graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, the Whisper models run on it through Vulkan.

What does my PC need?

Windows 11, or Windows 10 22H2, 64-bit, with an Intel or AMD processor with AVX2 (Intel Core 4th generation or newer, AMD Ryzen, Intel N100). 8 GB of RAM is recommended, and about 1 GB of free storage. No administrator rights needed.

Why does Windows warn me?

Vorto isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC”. Select More info, then Run anyway. The source code and the build are public on GitHub.

Is Vorto free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT license. Cloud AI providers bill you directly if you use them.
